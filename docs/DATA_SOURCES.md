## Usage policies we follow

- **User-Agent:** every pipeline request sends `wayfarer-pipeline/<version> (<PIPELINE_CONTACT_EMAIL>)`.
- **Rate limits:** Wikidata SPARQL (≤ 2 concurrent queries — WDQS allows 5 per client, we stay low on shared CI IPs — one query start per second, back off on 429 / `Retry-After`), Overpass (≤ 1 request at a time, honour `Retry-After`), Nominatim (≤ 1 request/second, never from the app), Wikimedia REST (≤ 100 req/s, we stay far below).
- **Caching:** raw responses cached under `data-pipeline/.cache/` during development (git-ignored).
- **No invented data:** missing fields stay `null` and the UI handles them.

## cities.yaml provenance

Retrieved 2026-09-26: Wikidata labels (en/pt), coordinates (P625) and country (P17 → P297); Nominatim search for bbox and OSM boundary relation. Istanbul uses a centre-based bbox because its OSM boundary is the whole province.

2026-09-28: 20 more cities (Berlin, Venice, Florence, Munich, Dublin, Athens, Budapest, Edinburgh, Brussels, Copenhagen, Stockholm, Nice, Seville, Kraków, Warsaw, Naples, Valencia, Zurich, Antalya, Oslo), chosen from European entries in the most-visited-city rankings (Euromonitor Top 100 City Destinations, Mastercard Global Destination Cities). Athens, Copenhagen and Antalya resolve to a place node in Nominatim, so they use its centre-based bbox (±0.16°) like Istanbul.

2026-09-28 (batch 3): Hamburg, Frankfurt, Cologne, Geneva, Salzburg, Bruges, Antwerp, Rotterdam, Helsinki, Reykjavík, Tallinn, Riga, Vilnius, Dubrovnik, Split, Ljubljana, Bratislava, Palma, Málaga, Bologna. Same method; Hamburg (boundary includes the North Sea island of Neuwerk) and Palma (boundary extends far out to sea) use a centre-based ±0.16° bbox. New destination countries FI, IS, EE, LV, LT, HR, SI, SK checked; LT (voltage, legacy police number) and SI (languages) overridden.

## Reference data provenance (retrieved 2026-09-27)

Generated by `data-pipeline` (see its README) into committed snapshots in `data-pipeline/data/` and seed SQL in `supabase/seed/`.

- **Countries** (`10_countries.sql`, 250 rows = 249 ISO 3166-1 codes + `XK` Kosovo): Wikidata SPARQL (P297 ISO code without end date, no P576; labels en/pt; P38→P498 currency; P2853 plug → IEC letter by QID; P2884 voltage + P2144 frequency; P1622 driving side; P474 calling code; P2852 emergency numbers with P366 use; P37→P218 languages), time zones from IANA `zone.tab`, EU/Schengen flags hardcoded (EU 27, Schengen 29). Curated corrections in `country_overrides.yaml` (each with a `source:`); launch destinations GB, FR, TR, IT, CZ, NL, ES, AT, PT and home countries BR, US are fully reviewed; the 2026-09-28 destinations DE, IE, GR, HU, BE, DK, SE, PL, CH, NO were checked (only GR/PL/SE languages needed overrides); batch 3 LT/SI (languages); batch 4 BG, CY, LU, MT, RO checked (only CY needed an override: 1400 is not an ambulance number; BG uses EUR since 2026-01-01). Brazil is mixed 127/220 V — stored as 220 V.
- **Visa** (`30_visa.sql`, 39 402 rows): `passport-index-tidy-iso2.csv` at commit `a46dbc872002e01f6465a69071a7ed5b6dc8fc7b` (2025-01-12; the repository was archived in 2026, so data is not refreshed upstream). Numbers → `visa_free` with `max_stay_days`; passport and destination both in EU+EEA+CH → `freedom_of_movement`.
- **Attractions** (`40_attractions.sql`): Wikidata (CC0) + OpenStreetMap via Overpass (ODbL) + Wikipedia pageviews (Sep 2025 – Aug 2026) + Commons image metadata. Only freely licensed images are kept, each with author, licence and licence URL.

## Refresh schedule (D-042)

- **Monthly** (`.github/workflows/data-refresh.yml`): countries, visa rules, city summaries and attractions are fetched again with the same queries and politeness rules as above. The data gate compares the result with main before a pull request opens. The provenance dates in this file are those of the last reviewed refresh.
- **Live** (checklist Edge Function): weather, exchange rates and advisories on every request, with a 3–24 h cache, checked weekly by `.github/workflows/providers-check.yml`.
- **Visa:** the upstream repository is archived, so refreshes return the 2025-01-12 data until a maintained source replaces it.

## Notable people provenance (retrieved 2026-10-05; D-071)

`python -m wayfarer_pipeline people --all` → `data-pipeline/data/people/<slug>.json` → `50_people.sql` (`notable_people`). Wikidata SPARQL (CC0): people with `P19` (place of birth) or `P20` (place of death) equal to the city item and at least 20 sitelinks (8 when that finds fewer than 12); their labels (EN, else `mul`; PT), descriptions, `P569`/`P570` years, `P18` portrait, en/pt Wikipedia sitelinks and `P106` occupations, mapped to categories through `P279*` roots (see D-071). Portraits: Wikimedia Commons `imageinfo` (author, licence, licence URL; non-free files dropped), shown with "Photo: {author} · {licence}". 198 cities, 4,143 people in 179 of them, 3,908 with a portrait; 1,082 requests. The city page credits "From Wikidata (CC0); photos from Wikimedia Commons".

## Suggested routes provenance (generated 2026-10-10; D-077)

`python -m wayfarer_pipeline suggest-routes --all` → `data-pipeline/data/suggested_routes/<slug>.json` → `60_suggested_routes.sql` (`suggested_routes`, `suggested_route_stops`). No new external source: a route is a selection and an order of attractions already in `data/attractions` (Wikidata CC0, OpenStreetMap ODbL, Commons), grouped by straight-line distance; walking distance and time use the app's walking model (straight line × 1.3, 4.5 km/h). Themes and titles are written by the rule-based planner (committed data) or by a local Ollama model (`--planner ollama`; the model and its licence are recorded per route in `generated_by` — check the model's licence before shipping its output). The pipeline's HTTP calls go only to the local Ollama server (`http://localhost:11434`). 198 cities, 1,141 routes.

## Article texts provenance (retrieved 2026-10-05, Europe; D-070)

`python -m wayfarer_pipeline city-summaries --region europe` and `attraction-texts --region europe` → `data-pipeline/data/city_summaries.json` (`summary_*`, `history_*`) and `data-pipeline/data/attraction_texts/<slug>.json` → `20_cities.sql`, `40_attractions.sql`. Each article is read once with `https://{en,pt}.wikipedia.org/w/api.php?action=query&prop=extracts|pageprops&explaintext=1&exsectionformat=wiki&redirects=1`; the introduction is the text before the first heading (up to 3,000 characters, whole paragraphs) and the History excerpt the paragraphs of the "History" / "História" section, subheadings dropped (up to 1,500 characters). Text is verbatim; texts with leftover wikitext and disambiguation pages are dropped. 105 cities (104 with an English and 89 with a Portuguese History excerpt) and 12,947 attractions (12,464 English and 3,262 Portuguese introductions; 7,087 English and 1,353 Portuguese History excerpts), 15,428 requests. CC BY-SA 4.0: the app shows "From Wikipedia · CC BY-SA 4.0" and **Read the full article on Wikipedia** under every text. Cities outside Europe keep the 2026-09-29 REST lead below until `city-summaries` runs for them.

## City summaries provenance (retrieved 2026-09-29)

`python -m wayfarer_pipeline city-summaries` → `data-pipeline/data/city_summaries.json` → `20_cities.sql` (`cities.summary_en/pt`, `wikipedia_en/pt`). Article titles are the city item's en/pt Wikipedia sitelinks (Wikidata SPARQL); the text is the `extract` of `https://{en,pt}.wikipedia.org/api/rest_v1/page/summary/{title}`, kept verbatim (trimmed), and only for `standard` pages (disambiguation pages are skipped). All 198 cities have an English lead and all but Huế a Portuguese one (Brașov's English lead is one 79-character sentence, which the data gate lists as short). CC BY-SA 4.0 requires attribution and a link, which the city page shows under the text; we do not modify the text. Portuguese Wikipedia mixes European and Brazilian spelling.
